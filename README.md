## 🎯 Why AccentoMaster?

Have you ever frozen mid-sentence, unsure whether to say *зво́нит* or *звони́т*? Have you ever been gently corrected by a native speaker and felt that familiar blush? You're not alone. Russian word stress is notoriously fluid — it shifts, dances, and refuses to follow predictable rules. 

**AccentoMaster** transforms this linguistic challenge into a delightful daily ritual. Instead of dry memorization, we offer an interactive playground where you'll train your ear, sharpen your intuition, and internalize the rhythm of Russian speech. This isn't just another flashcard app — it's a mindful practice space designed for lifelong learners.
